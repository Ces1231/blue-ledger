Launch Prep
48 Hours Before
Everything that must be done before the launch meeting.
Pre-launch checklist
48 hours prior
1
Seed all member profiles in the sheet
Every active member's Name, Email, Member #, Induction Year must be in the 👥 Users tab before they sign in. Export your current roster. Copy-paste into the sheet. This is the one manual step that takes the most time — do it now, not on launch day.
2
Add every member to the Glide allowlist
Settings → Sign In → Allowed Emails → paste all active member emails. Any email not on this list gets rejected at sign-in. Double-check for typos — a rejected sign-in at a chapter meeting is embarrassing.
3
Send the app announcement in the group chat
Use the announcement template below. Send 48 hours before the meeting — not the morning of. Brothers need time to download and sign in before the meeting starts.
4
Prepare a 10-minute demo slide or screen share
Plan your screen share: Home Dashboard → Digital ID → Leaderboard → Scanner demo. Practice it twice. Keep it under 10 minutes — brothers don't want a tutorial, they want to play with it themselves.
5
Set up a 'First Quest' badge moment
Create a custom announcement in the chapter chat: 'First 10 brothers to complete their full profile earn 100 XP + the Architect badge + a shoutout at the next meeting.' This drives immediate engagement before anyone even opens the app.

Launch Meeting
Meeting Day Runbook
10-minute launch demo — run at the chapter meeting
Present this, then let them explore
1
Open with the Leaderboard (pre-populated with E-Board data)
First screen they see: the Leaderboard already has 8–12 brothers on it from the beta test week. Their XP and avatars are visible. This immediately shows brothers what they're competing for — not what they have to set up.
2
Show your own Home Dashboard
Project your phone screen. Show: your name, XP, avatar, level progress bar. Navigate to Digital ID — show the QR code. Then hand the phone to the brother next to you and have them scan your ID using their phone's Scanner tab. Live demo of the core loop in under 90 seconds.
3
Show the Leaderboard updating in real time
Have a Chair scan 3 brothers while projected. Everyone watches their XP appear on the Leaderboard in real time. This is the moment that makes the app feel magical — they see points arriving before their eyes.
4
Announce the first Quest
'First 10 brothers to complete their full profile earn Architect badge + 100 XP. The clock starts now.' Pause the meeting for 5 minutes — let everyone sign in and complete profiles. Watch the Leaderboard shift in real time as points come in.
5
Post the leaderboard screenshot to the group chat
Immediately after the meeting, screenshot the Leaderboard — it should now have most of the chapter on it with real XP. Post it to the group chat. Tag the top 3 brothers. This creates FOMO for anyone who didn't complete their profile.

What's Next
Post-Launch: Year 2 Roadmap
Natural evolution after a successful launch
Plan for this in Year 2
→
Spring semester: first full semester of live data
By Spring 2026 you'll have a complete Fall 2025 dataset — attendance patterns, XP distribution, service hour trends, quiz pass rates. Use this to identify which brothers need outreach and which programs drove the most engagement.
→
Regional chapters want this
When brothers from other chapters see the app at regional events, they'll ask for it. Prepare a 'deployment kit': the Master Sheet template, this repository, and a link to the Glide config guide. You can share as a self-service package or help them set it up.
→
National org interest
The PIA Builder's automated export solves a real pain point at every level of the organization. If regional leadership sees it working, there's a genuine path to a national pilot. Start documenting your export data from Day 1.
→
Upgrade triggers
Move to Glide Maker ($49/mo) when: roster exceeds 500 rows, you need a custom domain, or you add a second chapter. Move to a custom React/Node app when: you want to charge other chapters, need real-time push notifications, or want full infrastructure ownership.
→
The codebase is already built
The BlueLedger_App.html prototype in this repo is a pixel-perfect developer spec. Any developer can open it, understand every screen and interaction, and build a production version. When you're ready to scale, the spec work is done.
